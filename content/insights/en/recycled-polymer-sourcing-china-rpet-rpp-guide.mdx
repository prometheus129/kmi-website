## Why China for Recycled Polymers

China's recycled polymer industry operates at a scale that no other country matches. The combination of massive domestic plastic waste collection infrastructure, established processing capacity, and growing export orientation makes China the default sourcing origin for buyers who need certified recycled compounds at volume.

For rPET specifically, China's collection and sorting infrastructure processes millions of tonnes of post-consumer PET bottles annually. The resulting rPET flakes and pellets feed both domestic demand — driven by China's own sustainability mandates — and a growing export market serving packaging, fiber, and automotive applications globally.

For rPP, the market is smaller but growing rapidly. Post-consumer and post-industrial PP recycling in China has expanded significantly as automotive manufacturers, home appliance producers, and packaging companies face their own recycled content requirements from brand customers and regulators.

The structural cost advantage that makes China competitive in virgin polymers — lower energy costs, labor efficiency, and scale economics — applies equally to recycled polymers. GRS-certified rPET and rPP from China typically price 20-40% below equivalent certified material from European recyclers.

## The Certification Landscape

Not all recycled polymers are equal. For converters facing EU PPWR compliance deadlines, brand sustainability mandates, or export market requirements, certification is the dividing line between usable supply and unusable supply.

### GRS (Global Recycled Standard)

The most widely held certification among Chinese recycled polymer producers. GRS requires:

- Minimum 50% recycled content (most producers exceed this significantly)
- Chain-of-custody traceability from post-consumer collection through processing
- Environmental management criteria (wastewater, emissions, chemical use)
- Social compliance (labor standards, safety)
- Third-party verification by accredited certifiers (Control Union, Intertek, TÜV SÜD, Ecocert)

**What buyers should verify:** A GRS certificate is issued to a specific facility for specific product categories. A supplier's GRS certificate for recycled PET flakes does not automatically cover recycled PET pellets or compounds produced at a different facility. Always request the Transaction Certificate (TC) for each shipment — this confirms the specific material in that transaction was produced under GRS-certified conditions.

### FDA / EFSA / LFGB Compliance

For recycled material entering food-contact packaging, additional certifications are required beyond GRS:

- **FDA (US)** — Letter of No Objection (LNO) for the specific recycling process
- **EFSA (EU)** — Favorable scientific opinion on the recycling process for food-contact use
- **LFGB (Germany)** — German food-contact compliance, widely accepted across EU

**Critical distinction:** GRS certifies the recycled content and chain of custody. FDA/EFSA/LFGB certify the material is safe for food contact. For food-contact packaging applications, you need both.

### REACH and RoHS

For converters exporting to the EU:

- **REACH** — Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals. Recycled polymers must comply with REACH substance restrictions. A REACH declaration from the supplier confirms the recycled material does not contain restricted substances above threshold levels.
- **RoHS 2.0** — Restriction of Hazardous Substances. Primarily relevant for electrical and electronic applications but increasingly requested by EU importers across sectors.

### What "Certified" Actually Means in Practice

The Chinese recycled polymer market includes producers at every level of certification maturity. When evaluating a potential supplier:

| Verification Level | What It Tells You | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|
| GRS certificate displayed on website | Facility has been certified at some point | **Medium** — certificates expire, scope may not cover your product |
| Current GRS certificate with valid date range | Facility is currently certified | **Lower** — still need to verify product scope |
| Transaction Certificate (TC) per shipment | The specific material you're buying was produced under GRS conditions | **Lowest** — this is the documentation your customer needs |
| No third-party certification, "self-declared recycled content" | Unverifiable claim | **High** — insufficient for PPWR compliance or brand mandates |

## rPET: What's Available from China

Chinese rPET production covers the full value chain from post-consumer bottle collection through to food-grade pellets.

### Product Forms

**rPET flakes (hot-washed)** — the most widely traded form. Post-consumer PET bottles are collected, sorted by color, shredded, hot-washed, and dried. Quality grades range from clear/blue bottle flakes (highest value, suitable for bottle-to-bottle recycling) to mixed-color flakes (lower value, used in fiber and sheet applications).

**rPET pellets** — flakes that have been further processed through extrusion and pelletization. Available in:
- *Fiber-grade* — for polyester fiber production (largest volume application)
- *Sheet-grade* — for thermoforming applications
- *Food-grade* — processed through FDA/EFSA-approved super-cleaning or solid-state polycondensation (SSP) processes. Commands a significant premium over non-food grades.

**rPET compounds** — pellets blended with additives, fillers, or other polymers to meet specific application requirements. Glass-fiber-reinforced rPET compounds are used in automotive and electronics applications.

### Key Quality Parameters

When sourcing rPET from China, these specifications determine usability:

| Parameter | Flakes (Hot-Washed) | Pellets (Fiber-Grade) | Pellets (Food-Grade) |
|---|---|---|---|
| IV (Intrinsic Viscosity) | 0.65-0.75 dl/g | 0.60-0.70 dl/g | 0.80-0.85 dl/g |
| Moisture | ≤1.0% | ≤0.05% | ≤0.05% |
| Color (b* value) | ≤3.0 (clear) | ≤3.0 | ≤2.0 |
| PVC content | ≤100 ppm | N/A | ≤50 ppm |
| Metal content | ≤20 ppm | ≤10 ppm | ≤5 ppm |
| Melting point | 245-255°C | 245-255°C | 248-255°C |

**IV (Intrinsic Viscosity)** is the single most important specification. It determines the mechanical strength of the final product. Food-grade rPET requires higher IV (0.80+) because the material must withstand bottle-blowing pressures. Lower-IV material is suitable for fiber and sheet applications.

## rPP: What's Available from China

The recycled PP market from China is less mature than rPET but expanding rapidly.

### Sources of Post-Consumer PP

Chinese rPP primarily comes from:
- **Post-consumer packaging** — PP containers, caps, and closures from household waste streams
- **Post-consumer automotive** — bumpers, interior panels, and battery cases from end-of-life vehicles
- **Post-industrial** — factory scrap from injection molding, extrusion, and thermoforming operations (note: post-industrial recyclate does not count toward PPWR targets)

### Product Forms

**rPP pellets (natural/gray)** — sorted, washed, and pelletized post-consumer PP. Color ranges from near-white (high-purity sorting) to medium gray (mixed sources). Natural-color rPP commands a premium.

**rPP compounds** — pelletized rPP blended with mineral fillers (talc, calcium carbonate), glass fiber reinforcement, or impact modifiers to achieve specific mechanical properties. Compounds are typically formulated to match virgin PP grade specifications for direct substitution.

**rPP masterbatch** — concentrated additive blends using rPP as a carrier resin. Used by converters who blend recycled content into their production at controlled ratios.

### Key Quality Parameters

| Parameter | rPP Pellets (General) | rPP Compound (Automotive) | rPP Compound (Packaging) |
|---|---|---|---|
| MFI (Melt Flow Index) | 4-25 g/10min | 8-15 g/10min | 10-30 g/10min |
| Tensile strength | ≥20 MPa | ≥25 MPa | ≥22 MPa |
| Impact strength (Izod) | ≥3 kJ/m² | ≥5 kJ/m² | ≥4 kJ/m² |
| Ash content | ≤3% | Varies (filler-dependent) | ≤2% |
| Moisture | ≤0.1% | ≤0.05% | ≤0.05% |
| Odor | Low | Low (automotive OEM spec) | Food-grade (minimal) |

**MFI consistency** is the critical quality indicator for rPP. Virgin PP has tight MFI specifications; recycled PP varies batch-to-batch because the feedstock mix changes. Producers who can maintain MFI within ±2 g/10min across batches are operating at a higher capability level.

## Sourcing Checklist: Before You Order

For converters evaluating Chinese recycled polymer suppliers for the first time, this checklist covers the essential verification steps.

### Documentation to Request

- Current GRS certificate (check valid date range and product scope)
- Transaction Certificate (TC) sample — confirms per-shipment traceability
- Technical Data Sheet (TDS) for the specific grade you need
- Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)
- FDA Letter of No Objection or EFSA opinion (if food-contact application)
- REACH compliance declaration (if exporting to EU)
- RoHS 2.0 test report (if required by your customer)
- Chain of custody documentation showing post-consumer origin

### Quality Verification Steps

1. **Request pre-shipment samples** — minimum 5 kg, from the same production batch as the intended shipment. Test IV (rPET), MFI (rPP), color, moisture, and contaminant levels against your specifications.

2. **Verify certification independently** — check the supplier's GRS certificate against the Textile Exchange certified supplier database. Certificates can be fabricated; database entries cannot.

3. **Ask about feedstock consistency** — where does the post-consumer material come from? A supplier sourcing from a consistent collection network will deliver more consistent quality than one buying spot-market feedstock.

4. **Check production capacity vs. your volume** — a supplier producing 500 MT/month can reliably supply your 30 MT/month order. A supplier producing 50 MT/month may prioritize larger customers when supply is tight.

5. **Request a factory audit** — for orders above 50 MT/month or for food-contact applications, an on-site audit (or third-party audit report) is standard practice. SGS, Bureau Veritas, and Intertek all offer recycled polymer supplier audits in China.

### Pricing Considerations

Recycled polymer pricing from China is less transparent than virgin polymer pricing. There is no ICIS benchmark for recycled compounds. Key factors that affect pricing:

- **Certification premium** — GRS-certified material typically commands 5-15% above uncertified equivalent grades
- **Color** — clear/natural rPET and white/natural rPP command 10-20% premium over gray or mixed-color grades
- **Food-contact approval** — FDA/EFSA-approved recycled material commands 15-25% premium over non-food grades
- **Post-consumer vs. post-industrial** — post-consumer recyclate (required for PPWR) costs more than post-industrial due to higher processing costs
- **Volume commitment** — annual contracts with volume commitments typically yield 3-8% better pricing than spot purchases

| Shandong (Linyi, Zibo) | Qingdao | rPET flakes (high volume) |
| Fujian (Quanzhou) | Xiamen | rPET fiber-grade pellets |

### Trade Terms

Standard trade terms for recycled polymer exports from China follow the same structure as virgin polymers:

- **FOB (Free on Board)** — most common. Buyer arranges freight. Price quoted per metric tonne at the loading port.
- **CFR (Cost and Freight)** — seller includes ocean freight to destination port. Useful when the seller has better freight rates.
- **Payment terms** — typically 30% deposit against proforma invoice, 70% against copy of Bill of Lading (B/L). For established relationships, full payment against B/L or 30-60 day credit terms are negotiable.

### HS Code Classification

Recycled polymers use the same HS codes as virgin polymers in most markets. The key codes:

| Material | HS Code | Description |
|---|---|---|
| rPET flakes | 3907.61 | PET with viscosity ≥78 ml/g |
| rPET pellets | 3907.69 | Other PET |
| rPP pellets | 3902.10 | Polypropylene, primary forms |
| rPP compounds | 3902.30 | PP copolymers, primary forms |

**Important:** Some countries have specific HS codes or sub-codes for recycled polymers that may carry different duty rates or regulatory requirements. Verify the applicable HS code and duty rate in your destination country before finalizing pricing.

## The Market Access Gap

The certified recycled polymer supply in China is large and growing. The demand from non-EU converters facing PPWR deadlines, brand mandates, and domestic EPR requirements is accelerating.

What remains underdeveloped is the market access layer between them — the sourcing infrastructure that connects a GRS-certified Chinese recycler producing 500 MT/month of food-grade rPET with a packaging converter in Istanbul who needs 30 MT/month with full chain-of-custody documentation, REACH compliance, and consistent quality.

B2B platforms list products but cannot verify certifications in real time. Trading houses focus on virgin polymers where margins are established. Local agents lack the certification expertise to evaluate recycled compound quality across the full supply chain.

For converters who need certified recycled supply to maintain EU market access, the sourcing challenge is real — and it rewards those who start building relationships before the compliance deadlines make everyone a buyer at the same time.
