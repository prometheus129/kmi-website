## Indonesia Imports Almost All Its Engineering Polymer Demand — and China Can Land Duty-Free with Form E

Indonesia is Southeast Asia's largest polymer market, but its domestic petrochemical base — led by Chandra Asri — is built for commodity olefins: polyethylene and polypropylene. Engineering polymers are a different story. There is minimal to no domestic virgin production of PA6, PA66, polycarbonate, POM or PBT, so the large majority of the country's engineering polymer demand is imported. That demand has historically been met by Western, Korean, Japanese and Taiwanese suppliers at a 15–70% price premium over Chinese equivalents.

Two structural facts make China-origin engineering polymer competitive into Indonesia. First, duty: engineering polymer resins in primary form generally qualify for **0% under the ASEAN–China Free Trade Agreement (ACFTA) with a valid Form E** certificate of origin — the same Normal-Track preferential regime that already zeroes commodity PE, PP and PVC — where Indonesia's applied MFN would otherwise be positive (its average applied MFN is around 8%). Because concessions are set per HS subheading, confirm yours against the Indonesia National Single Window (INSW) rather than assuming a blanket rate. Second, logistics: **Shanghai or Ningbo to Tanjung Priok runs about 7–10 days on frequent direct services**, materially shorter than Gulf Coast, Korean or Middle East routes. With Form-E duty relief and short transit, most of the 15–70% Western premium becomes available as landed-cost saving (net of freight and the cash-flow cost of PPN) — widest on POM (50–70%) and PA66 (30–50%). The practical implication for a distributor: the first switch to evaluate is usually POM or PA66 — the widest premiums, with PA66 helped further by a Chinese oversupply pushing prices down — not "Chinese engineering polymer" as a category.

> **Quick summary — key points:**
> - **0% ACFTA duty** on China-origin engineering polymer resin with Form E (PA6/PA66 HS 3908, PC 3907.40, ABS 3903.30, POM 3907.10, PBT 3907.99) — confirm the specific subheading against INSW.
> - **No anti-dumping duty** in force on China-origin PA6, PA66, PC, ABS or POM. Indonesia's active/pending polymer cases are on PP homopolymer (KADI has proposed duties, not yet enacted) and BOPP film — neither touches engineering polymers.
> - **PPN (VAT) effective 11%** on CIF-plus-duty — statutory 12% applied on an 11/12 base for non-luxury goods (PMK 131/2024); creditable for registered importers (a cash-flow cost, not permanent).
> - **Import dependency:** minimal domestic engineering-polymer production; the segment is structurally import-led.
> - **Western premium:** ~20–35% PA6; 30–50% PA66; 15–30% PC; 10–25% ABS; **50–70% POM (widest)**; 25–40% PBT.
> - **Primary Chinese producers:** Shenma (PA66, largest integrated), Wanhua (PC CLARNATE), CNPC Jilin (~400 KT/yr ABS), Yuntianhua (POM M-series), Chang Chun/Sinopec Yizheng (PBT), Sinopec/Kingfa (PA6).
> - **Custom compounding via Remay** covers glass-fibre, flame-retardant and colour-matched formulations that off-the-shelf grades cannot.

## The Import Regime for Engineering Polymers in Indonesia

**Duty — 0% ACFTA with Form E.** The main engineering polymer families in primary form generally sit on the ACFTA concession schedule at 0% for China origin with a valid Form E. Because concessions are defined per HS subheading, the discipline is to confirm the exact subheading — not to assume a blanket rate across all polymers. An FTA existing is not proof that a specific grade benefits; verify against INSW (insw.go.id) for the subheading on your commercial invoice.

**PPN (VAT) — effective 11%.** Indonesia's statutory PPN is 12%, but non-luxury goods including polymer resin are charged on an 11/12 base, giving an effective 11% (PMK 131/2024); the full 12% applies only to luxury goods. Applied on the CIF-plus-duty value and creditable against output VAT for PKP-registered importers, so it inflates the initial cash outlay at clearance but is recovered on the monthly return. Plan working capital around this cycle, not around a duty that, with a qualifying Form E, is 0%.

**Anti-dumping — none on the engineering set.** No BMAD is in force on China-origin PA6, PA66, PC, ABS or POM. The active and pending Indonesian polymer cases are KADI's proposed anti-dumping duties on PP homopolymer (investigation initiated December 2024, covering several origins including China; proposed rates roughly 5.5–36.6%, not yet enacted) and the in-force BOPP film duty under PMK 60/2024 (finished film, not resin). Monitor kadi.kemendag.go.id for changes before committing volume.

**Standards — check SNI applicability.** Indonesia applies mandatory SNI (Standar Nasional Indonesia) certification to certain products. Whether it applies to your import depends on the specific grade and end-use, so confirm SNI status for your finished product rather than assuming resin-level exemption.

## Chinese Producer Landscape by Polymer Family

The producer and grade facts below are properties of the Chinese supply base, not of any single destination market — they hold for Indonesia the same as for any importing country.

**PA6 (Nylon 6):** Sinopec leads commodity engineering-grade PA6 (PA6-1013B and related grades directionally match DuPont Zytel 101L NC010 and BASF Ultramid B3S on melt-flow and density). Kingfa produces both base resin and compounds.

**PA66 (Nylon 66):** Shenma Group is China's largest integrated PA66 producer (adipic acid through polymer), ranked by industry analysts alongside INVISTA among the primary Chinese PA66 names. Unfilled grades directionally match DuPont Zytel base; GF-reinforced automotive grades (PA66-GF33) directionally match DuPont Zytel 70G33L. A structural China PA66 oversupply — roughly 70% capacity added 2022–2025, to about 1.5M MT nameplate — puts durable downward pressure on Chinese PA66 pricing through 2026–27.

**PC (Polycarbonate):** Wanhua Chemical (Yantai) is the clear Chinese leader, marketing the CLARNATE A-series. A1105 gives approximate equivalence to Covestro Makrolon 2405 (MVR ~12); A1225 to LED-diffusion grades (MVR ~22–25). For optical, food-contact and flame-retardant PC, Remay custom compounding covers the specialty tier.

**ABS:** CNPC Jilin Petrochemical is the largest Chinese producer (~400,000 MT/yr at Jilin plus Jieyang). Grade 0215A (MFI ~15–18) directionally matches LG Chem Starex SD-0150 and INEOS Styrolution Terluran GP-22 for general-purpose high-flow injection.

**POM (Acetal):** Yuntianhua Group is China's primary producer. M-series copolymer directionally matches Celanese Hostaform MFI ranges — M90 (MFI ~9) to Hostaform C 9021; M270 (MFI ~27) to C 27021. POM carries the widest Western premium in the set (50–70%) — the largest per-ton saving of any grade here, though the total opportunity for a given converter also turns on its POM volume and requalification path.

**PBT:** Chang Chun Group and Sinopec Yizheng (~91,000 MT/yr) are the primary sources; 1100/6330 series directionally match DuPont Crastin and BASF Ultradur unfilled grades. Chang Chun manufactures across several countries — verify the country of origin on the invoice, since it drives the Form E claim.

## Application Clusters — Where the Thesis Applies in Indonesia

*Cluster mappings are qualitative and describe known industrial footprints; treat any volume figure as an estimate pending verification.*

**Automotive — Karawang / Bekasi / Cikampek (West Java).** Indonesia is one of Southeast Asia's largest vehicle producers, anchored by Toyota, Daihatsu, Mitsubishi, Honda and Suzuki plants across the West Java corridor. Engineering-polymer demand centres on PA66-GF (under-hood), POM (fuel-system and mechanical parts), PBT (electrical connectors) and PC/ABS (interior). Tier 2/3 component suppliers under OEM cost-down pressure are the most likely group to evaluate China-origin resin and compound.

**Appliances and electronics.** Multinational makers with Greater-Jakarta plants — Sharp, Panasonic, LG, Samsung across the Bekasi/Cikarang/Tangerang belt — plus major domestic manufacturers Polytron (Central Java) and Maspion (East Java) drive ABS and PC demand for housings and PA/PBT for connectors and internal components. Appliance premiumisation and electronics assembly growth support rising engineering-polymer volume.

**General industrial and East Java — Surabaya / Gresik / Sidoarjo.** Indonesia's second industrial hub adds POM, PA and PBT demand across mechanical, electrical and consumer-goods manufacturing, served through Tanjung Perak.

## Remay Custom Compounding — The Differentiator

Off-the-shelf Chinese grades cover general-purpose needs. Where an Indonesian converter needs a specific formulation — "PA66-GF33 in RAL 7035 with UL94 V-0 and HDT above 250 °C" — that is a compounding order, not a commodity resin order. Remay (Shenzhen), KMI's compounding partner, produces to formulation across PA6, PA66, PC, ABS, PC/ABS alloy, POM, PBT, PPS, PPA, LCP and thermoplastic elastomers, with glass-fibre (GF10–GF50), flame-retardant (UL94 V-0/5V), heat-stabilised, UV-stabilised and colour-matched variants. No commodity trader can offer single-source custom compounding — it is a higher-value service tier priced on formulation engineering, not material cost alone. There is also an access point here that is easy to miss: a mid-tier Indonesian distributor ordering on its own volume rarely secures producer-direct grade access or single-lot custom compounding from a Shenma or a Wanhua. Pooled multi-buyer demand is what unlocks both — the value the incumbent Western-supplier chain has quietly kept priced into that 15–70% premium.

## When to Stay Western, When to Switch

**Stay Western when** the grade is OEM-approved-vendor-list-locked at a Tier 1 program and requalification risk outweighs the saving; the application needs a specific tested additive package with a long track record; regulatory compliance requires brand-specific certification; or annual volume is small enough (under roughly 5–10 MT/yr per grade) that onboarding cost dominates.

**Switch to China when** the application is industrial or consumer-durable with no formal AVL lock-in; requalification is feasible (3–12 months); volume justifies onboarding (50+ MT/yr typical break-even); and the grade matches cleanly to a Chinese specification.

**Dual-source is the common Year 1 path:** run China-origin for 20–30% of volume while the incumbent holds the AVL position, build a consistency track record, then scale share in Year 2 with formal requalification on locked grades.
