## Why China-Origin Polymers Matter for Bangladesh

Bangladesh has effectively zero domestic polymer production capacity. PP, HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE, PVC, and PA6 — every kilogram of plastic raw material must be imported. Combined demand from the country's $40 billion-plus ready-made garment export sector, a rapidly expanding packaging industry, and infrastructure construction makes Bangladesh one of South Asia's largest polymer importers.

China is the dominant origin, supplying an estimated 36-40% of total polymer imports. The structural drivers are clear: CTO (Coal-to-Olefins) and PDH (Propane Dehydrogenation) feedstock routes give Chinese producers a cost advantage, APTA preferential tariffs reduce the customs duty burden, and transit times from Shanghai/Ningbo to Chittagong run 12-16 days.

However, incorrect tariff classification, documentation gaps, or banking process errors can substantially inflate import costs. This guide covers every step of importing plastic raw materials to Bangladesh — from HS codes and duty structure through the LC process and Chittagong port customs clearance.

## HS Codes and Import Duty Structure

Polymer resins in Bangladesh are classified under Chapter 39 (Plastics and Articles Thereof). Import duty rates for polymers in primary forms — granules, powder, flakes — under the FY 2025-26 tariff schedule are as follows:

| Product | HS Code | CD | SD | VAT | AIT | TTI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LDPE/LLDPE (SG < 0.94) | 3901.10.00 | 5% | 0% | 15% | 5% | 33.63% |
| HDPE (SG ≥ 0.94) | 3901.20.00 | 5% | 0% | 15% | 5% | 33.63% |
| PP (homopolymer) | 3902.10.00 | 5% | 0% | 15% | 5% | 33.63% |
| PP (copolymer) | 3902.30.00 | 5% | 0% | 15% | 5% | 33.63% |
| PVC (unplasticized, primary) | 3904.10.00 | 5% | 0% | 15% | 5% | 33.63% |
| PVC (plasticized) | 3904.21.00 | 10% | 0% | 15% | 5% | 39.95% |
| PA6 / Nylon 6 | 3908.10.00 | 5% | 0% | 15% | 5% | 33.63% |

**Key notes:**

- **CD (Customs Duty):** 5% is the standard rate for most commodity polymers in primary form. Plasticized PVC and certain finished articles attract rates of 10-25%.
- **TTI (Total Tax Incidence):** Beyond CD, VAT, and AIT, additional levies including RD (Regulatory Duty), AT (Advance Tax), and other minor charges may apply. The 33.63% TTI represents the combined effective rate of all components per NBR calculations. VAT and AIT cascade: VAT is computed on (AV + CD + SD) x 15%. Actual TTI may vary slightly between shipments.
- **VAT and AIT are creditable:** For registered businesses, import-stage VAT (15%) is recoverable as input tax credit, and AIT (5%) is adjustable against income tax liability.
- The final two digits of the HS code are Bangladesh-specific — confirm final classification against the NBR tariff schedule.

## APTA Tariff Benefits: Preferential Rates from China

Bangladesh is not an ASEAN member, so the ASEAN-China FTA (ACFTA) does not apply directly. The two principal avenues for preferential duty on China-origin imports are:

**1. APTA (Asia-Pacific Trade Agreement).** Both Bangladesh and China are APTA members. As an LDC (Least Developed Country), Bangladesh receives enhanced preferences — the average Margin of Preference (MoP) reaches up to 81%. Following fourth-round negotiations (effective from 2018), approximately 10,677 tariff lines carry preferential rates.

**2. China's DFQF scheme.** China extends Duty-Free Quota-Free (DFQF) access to LDC countries, covering 8,256 tariff lines (97% coverage) with zero-duty entry. However, this scheme applies to Bangladesh's exports to China, not to imports from China.

**APTA Certificate of Origin process:**

- The Chinese exporter obtains an APTA Certificate of Origin from China's customs authority
- At least 35% of the product's value must be added in China (reduced from 45% under the LDC concession)
- The certificate is valid for 12 months from the B/L date
- HS codes must match between the certificate and the B/L

**Important caveat — LDC graduation:** Bangladesh is in the LDC graduation process as of 2026. Following graduation, LDC-specific additional preferences under APTA may be lost. Importers should monitor this transition closely.

## Landed Cost Analysis

Understanding the true cost from FOB price to warehouse gate requires accounting for every cost layer. The following is an **illustrative calculation** — actual costs will vary with market conditions.

**Assumptions:**
- Product: HDPE pipe grade, FOB Ningbo
- Illustrative CFR market assessment: $1,000/MT
- Container: 20ft, approximately 22 MT
- Insurance: 0.3% of CFR value
- Duties: MFN rates (CD 5%, no preferential reduction)

| Cost Component | Per MT (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CFR Chittagong (illustrative) | $1,000 | Market assessment |
| Insurance (0.3% of CFR) | $3 | Marine cargo insurance |
| **CIF Chittagong (assessment value)** | **$1,003** | Duty base value |
| Customs Duty / CD (5%) | $50.15 | CIF x 5% |
| VAT (15%) | $157.97 | (CIF + CD) x 15% |
| AIT (5%) | $50.15 | CIF x 5% |
| C&F agent fee | $8-12 | Customs brokerage |
| THC and port charges | $10-15 | Terminal handling |
| Documentation fees (D/O, manifest) | $5-8 | Carrier and agent fees |
| Transport (Chittagong to Dhaka) | $15-20 | Truck freight |
| **Total landed cost (including taxes)** | **~$1,299-1,316** | |
| **Total landed cost (net of creditable taxes)** | **~$1,091-1,108** | After VAT and AIT adjustment |

**Analysis:** From a $1,000/MT CFR price, the actual uplift to warehouse gate — net of creditable taxes — is approximately $91-108/MT (9.1-10.8%). Customs duty at 5% is the largest component. Securing APTA preferential rates reduces the CD burden, making this the highest-leverage cost optimization available.

## LC Process and Bangladesh Bank Regulations

Letter of Credit (LC) is the primary payment instrument for polymer raw material imports into Bangladesh. The process is governed by Bangladesh Bank's Foreign Exchange Guidelines.

**Requirements for opening an LC:**

1. **IRC (Import Registration Certificate):** A valid IRC issued by CCI&E (Chief Controller of Imports & Exports) is mandatory. Processing typically takes 3 working days. Annual renewal is required (July-September).
2. **LCA Form (LC Authorization Form):** A separate LCA form must be completed for each LC. Correct HS code entry is mandatory — an incorrect HS code can trigger customs penalties.
3. **Bank solvency certificate:** The AD (Authorized Dealer) bank verifies the importer's financial capacity.
4. **Proforma Invoice / Purchase Agreement:** Contract document from the Chinese supplier.
5. **Insurance cover note:** Marine cargo insurance.
6. **TIN and VAT registration certificates.**

**Standard LC terms:**

| Item | Typical Terms |
|---|---|
| LC type | Irrevocable L/C at sight (predominant) |
| Margin / collateral | 10-50% (based on bank-importer relationship) |
| Currency | USD (predominant), CNY in some cases |
| LCA validity | 1 year (18 months for capital machinery) |
| Documents | B/L, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Insurance Certificate |

**Important:** Bangladesh Bank monitors all import transactions through its Online Import Monitoring System. AD banks submit reports on foreign currency transactions. Discrepancies trigger investigation.

## BDT/USD Foreign Exchange Risk Management

The Bangladeshi Taka has experienced persistent depreciation pressure against the US dollar. For polymer importers purchasing in USD, FX movement between LC opening and settlement can materially affect landed cost. Several practical strategies can mitigate exposure:

**Lock FX at LC opening.** When possible, secure dollar allocation from your AD bank at the time of LC issuance rather than at settlement. The gap between LC opening and cargo arrival (4-8 weeks) is long enough for meaningful BDT depreciation.

**Build FX buffer into landed cost models.** When modeling landed costs, include a 2-4% FX buffer to account for BDT movement between order and delivery. In periods of acute dollar scarcity, this buffer may need to be larger.

**Monitor Bangladesh Bank policy signals.** Bangladesh Bank periodically adjusts the interbank exchange rate corridor and dollar allocation mechanisms. Policy shifts can cause rapid BDT movement. Track BB circulars and adjust procurement timing accordingly.

**Consider multiple banking relationships.** Dollar availability varies across Bangladeshi commercial banks. Importers with AD relationships at multiple banks have more options for sourcing LC dollar allocation, particularly during periods of tight dollar supply.

**Negotiate fixed-validity CFR quotes.** Ensure supplier quotations have a clear validity period (typically 5-7 days). This gives time to arrange dollar funding without exposure to price revision.

## Chittagong Port: Customs Clearance Process

Chittagong port handles approximately 90% of Bangladesh's total imports. For polymer resin imports, it is the primary gateway.

**Clearance steps:**

1. **Appoint a C&F agent.** An approved C&F (Clearing and Forwarding) agent registered with the Chittagong Customs Clearing and Forwarding Agents Association is required. The agent manages customs declarations, duty payments, and cargo release.

2. **ASYCUDA declaration.** Electronic declaration via the ASYCUDA World system is mandatory at Chittagong port. Handwritten declarations are not accepted.

3. **Customs valuation.** Customs officers verify the declared CIF value. The transaction value method is primary — but if the declared value appears unusually low, customs may apply benchmark pricing.

4. **Cargo examination.** Under the risk-based system, some shipments receive direct clearance (Green Channel) while others undergo physical inspection.

5. **Duty payment and release.** Cargo release is granted after all duties and taxes are paid.

**Estimated timelines:**

| Step | Duration |
|---|---|
| Vessel arrival to document submission | 1-2 days |
| Customs assessment and duty determination | 1-3 days |
| Duty payment | 1 day |
| Cargo release (Green Channel) | 1-2 days |
| Cargo release (physical inspection required) | 3-7 days |
| **Total (normal conditions)** | **4-8 working days** |

**Common causes of delay:** HS code mismatch, document deficiency, Chittagong port congestion (particularly ahead of Eid), and customs valuation disputes.

## Common Errors and Risks

The following errors occur repeatedly in Bangladeshi polymer raw material imports:

**1. HS code misclassification:**
- LDPE and LLDPE both fall under 3901.10 (SG < 0.94), but some importers incorrectly classify LLDPE under 3901.90.
- PVC compound (mixed) and PVC resin (unmixed) carry different HS codes and different duty rates. Compounds under 3904.21 attract 10% CD.
- PP homopolymer (3902.10) and PP copolymer (3902.30) are distinct classifications — mixing them causes clearance delays.

**2. APTA certificate issues:**
- The Certificate of Origin must match the HS code, product description, and B/L information. Any discrepancy can void the preferential rate.
- An expired certificate results in MFN duty rates being applied.
- Failure to meet Rules of Origin (value addition requirement) renders the preference inapplicable.

**3. LC-related errors:**
- Incorrect HS code on the LCA form — causes customs penalties and clearance delays.
- Mismatch in product description, quantity, or value between the Proforma Invoice and the LC — the bank flags document discrepancies, adding time and cost.
- LC without insurance cover note — marine cargo insurance is mandatory under Bangladesh Bank regulations for CIF-terms imports.

**4. Customs valuation risk:**
- If the declared CIF value falls significantly below the customs benchmark, customs may impose its own valuation, increasing the actual duty burden.

**5. IRC renewal oversight:**
- An expired IRC prevents the opening of new LCs. Renew annually during the July-September window.

## IRC Registration: Steps and Requirements

Any business entity importing polymers into Bangladesh requires an Import Registration Certificate (IRC) from CCI&E.

**Required documents:**
- Certified copy of valid trade license
- Chamber of Commerce or relevant trade association membership certificate (BPGMEA or equivalent for plastic products)
- VAT registration certificate
- TIN certificate
- Bank solvency certificate
- Company incorporation certificate (for limited companies)
- Director's national identity card

**Processing time:** Typically 3 working days. Applications are submitted on the 3rd floor of CCI&E. The prescribed fee is deposited at Sonali Bank or Bangladesh Bank, and a Treasury Challan is collected.

## Pre-Shipment Checklist

Verify the following before your next polymer raw material import shipment:

1. **Confirm IRC validity** — an expired IRC prevents LC opening. Renewal window: July-September each year.

2. **Verify the HS code** — confirm the correct 8-digit code from the NBR tariff schedule. Use the same code across the LCA form, LC, Commercial Invoice, and Certificate of Origin.

3. **Secure the APTA certificate** — request that the Chinese supplier obtains an APTA Certificate of Origin from China Customs. Verify that the HS code, product description, and B/L details match.

4. **Check LC document consistency** — product description, quantity, and value must match across the Proforma Invoice, LC, and B/L.

5. **Confirm the C&F agent in advance** — contact an approved agent at Chittagong port before vessel arrival. Send all documents ahead of time.

6. **Arrange insurance early** — marine cargo insurance is mandatory under Bangladesh Bank regulations for CIF-terms imports.

7. **Prepare for customs valuation** — maintain recent market price documentation to support the declared CIF value.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the total duty on polymer imports into Bangladesh?**

Most commodity polymer resins (PP, HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE, PVC resin) carry CD at 5%, VAT at 15%, and AIT at 5%, resulting in a Total Tax Incidence (TTI) of approximately 33.63%. However, VAT and AIT are creditable — for registered businesses, the actual irrecoverable cost is primarily the CD (5%) plus ancillary fees.

**Are there preferential duty rates for polymer imports from China?**

Yes. Under APTA (Asia-Pacific Trade Agreement), preferential customs duty rates apply to imports from China. This requires the Chinese exporter to provide an APTA Certificate of Origin. Without the certificate, standard MFN rates apply (5% CD for most commodity polymers in primary form; 10-25% for plasticized PVC and certain finished articles).

**How long does polymer clearance take at Chittagong port?**

Under normal conditions, 4-8 working days. Green Channel clearance takes 1-2 days; shipments requiring physical inspection take 3-7 days. Delays may occur due to pre-Eid congestion, HS code mismatches, or document deficiencies.

**Is an IRC mandatory for wholesale polymer imports?**

Yes. A valid Import Registration Certificate (IRC) from CCI&E is required to open an LC. IRC requires annual renewal (July-September). Processing time is typically 3 working days.
