## PEEK Grade Families: Selecting the Right Compound

PEEK is a platform with distinct grade families. Selecting the wrong grade wastes qualification time — understanding the families before approaching any supplier prevents this.

### Neat (Unfilled) PEEK

The base polymer without reinforcement. Continuous service at 260°C, inherent UL 94 V-0 at thin sections without additives, and excellent chemical resistance against virtually every industrial solvent, acid, and hydrocarbon.

**Applications:** Oil and gas seals, backup rings, and valve seats. Medical implant components (spinal fusion cages, dental abutments). Semiconductor wet-bench fixtures and chemical handling. Bearing cages where self-lubricity matters.

**What to verify:** Melt viscosity consistency (PEEK processing is sensitive to molecular weight variation), crystallinity control (affects mechanical properties and dimensional stability), and purity levels (semiconductor and medical applications demand low extractables).

### Glass-Fiber Reinforced PEEK (GF10-GF30)

Glass fiber loading increases stiffness and dimensional stability while reducing thermal expansion. The trade-off is lower elongation at break and higher wear on mating surfaces. For a deep dive on GF loading selection across all polymer families, see our [glass fiber reinforced compounds guide](/insights/glass-fiber-reinforced-polymers-china-gf-compounds).

**Applications:** Structural brackets in aerospace and automotive for metal replacement. Connector housings in high-temperature electronics. Pump components and impellers requiring dimensional stability under thermal cycling.

**What to verify:** Fiber-matrix adhesion (poor coupling produces brittle parts), fiber length distribution (affects strength uniformity), and weld line strength (expect 70-85% of base strength at weld lines for well-formulated compounds).

### Carbon-Fiber Reinforced PEEK (CF10-CF30)

Carbon fiber delivers the highest stiffness-to-weight ratio in the PEEK family. CF-PEEK at 30% loading delivers flexural modulus of 20-24 GPa — approaching aluminum in specific stiffness while retaining PEEK's chemical resistance and thermal stability.

**Applications:** Aerospace structural components where weight reduction drives design. Semiconductor CMP retaining rings. High-performance bearing and seal applications requiring both low friction and high load capacity.

**What to verify:** Carbon fiber type (PAN-based vs. pitch-based affects properties), fiber-matrix interface quality (surface treatment is critical), and electrostatic discharge behavior (carbon fiber makes PEEK conductive — verify whether your application requires or prohibits this).

### Bearing and Wear Grades

Specialized compounds with PTFE, graphite, or carbon fiber blended for tribological applications, optimizing coefficient of friction and PV limits rather than raw strength.

**Typical formulations:** PEEK + 10% PTFE + 10% CF (general-purpose bearing). PEEK + 15% graphite + 15% CF (high-load, low-speed). PEEK + 30% PTFE (maximum lubricity).

**Applications:** Compressor components, pump wear rings, thrust washers, and guide bushings in aggressive chemical environments.

## Chinese PEEK Suppliers: What Is Available Today

China's PEEK industry has reached export readiness. Multiple producers now hold international certifications (UL Yellow Card, ISO 9001, select grades with FDA and REACH compliance) and are actively quoting international buyers. Understanding where Chinese PEEK is strongest — and where caution is warranted — prevents both over-caution and over-confidence.

### Where Chinese PEEK Is Strongest

**Semiconductor components.** This is the most mature Chinese PEEK application. China's semiconductor equipment buildout created intensive demand for wafer carriers, CMP rings, wet-bench fixtures, and handling components — driving producers to develop grades optimized for semiconductor purity requirements.

**Compounded grades (GF, CF, bearing).** Chinese compounders operate world-class compounding infrastructure — twin-screw extruders with gravimetric feeding, in-line quality monitoring — for formulating PEEK compounds with glass fiber, carbon fiber, PTFE, and graphite. Standard reinforced and bearing-grade PEEK from leading Chinese facilities matches published Western datasheets.

**Standard industrial applications.** Seals, valve seats, pump components, electrical insulators — well-defined specifications with extensive Chinese production experience.

### Where Caution Is Warranted

**Aerospace-qualified grades.** Western PEEK suppliers hold aerospace material specifications (AMS, ASTM) with decades of flight heritage. Chinese producers are building toward this but certification timelines are measured in years. For traceable aerospace certification with flight heritage, Western sources remain the established path today.

**Medical implant grades.** Permanent implant PEEK (spinal cages, cranial plates) requires ISO 10993 biocompatibility data packages, regulatory filings, and clinical history. The regulatory pathway is deliberately slow. Verify specific approvals — do not assume industrial-grade PEEK meets implant requirements.

**Ultra-high-purity semiconductor grades.** Front-end wafer processing and EUV lithography components require parts-per-billion contamination levels. Verify extractable testing data against your specific contamination specifications.

## How to Qualify a Chinese PEEK Supplier

PEEK applications are typically high-stakes. The qualification protocol must be proportionally rigorous. For [UL and REACH certification verification](/insights/ul-reach-fda-certification-switching-polymer-suppliers), see our dedicated certification guide.

### Step 1: Verify Certifications Against Your Requirements

| Application | Required Certifications | How to Verify |
|------------|------------------------|---------------|
| Electrical/electronic | UL Yellow Card (UL 94, RTI, CTI) | iq.ulprospector.com — verify file number, grade, property values |
| Food contact | FDA 21 CFR 177.2415 | Request FDA compliance letter with specific regulation reference |
| EU chemical compliance | REACH registration | Verify registration number on ECHA database |
| Medical device | ISO 10993 biocompatibility | Request full test reports — not just a declaration |
| Semiconductor | Extractable/leachable data | Request reports per your fab's contamination specs |

### Step 2: Material Property Verification

Request datasheets and COAs from the Chinese supplier. Compare property-by-property against the Western incumbent:

**Thermal (non-negotiable):** Tg should be ~143°C, Tm should be ~343°C. Any deviation indicates different polymer chemistry. HDT at 1.8 MPa must match.

**Mechanical:** Tensile strength, flexural modulus, elongation at break, notched Izod impact. All values within published specification range. For reinforced grades, verify both flow and cross-flow properties.

**Rheological:** Melt flow rate at standard conditions. Significant deviation means different processing behavior in your molds.

### Step 3: Batch Consistency

Request COAs from minimum 5 production batches. Target thresholds:
- Tensile strength: CV should be &lt; 3%
- Melt flow rate: CV should be &lt; 5%
- Color (if applicable): Delta-E &lt; 1.0 between batches

### Step 4: Application-Specific Testing

- **Injection molding:** Run trial shots. Adjust barrel temperatures, injection speed, and mold temperature — PEEK processing parameters are grade-specific.
- **Machining:** Verify surface finish, chip behavior, dimensional tolerance after machining and annealing.
- **Aging:** For long-service-life applications, run accelerated aging under your specific conditions (temperature, chemical exposure, mechanical load).

## Red Flags: When to Walk Away from a PEEK Supplier

Not all Chinese PEEK producers are equal. These warning signs indicate a supplier that is not ready for your application:

1. **No UL Yellow Card for the specific grade you are quoting.** A card for a different grade or a different polymer is not transferable. Verify the exact grade at iq.ulprospector.com.
2. **Refuses to provide multi-batch COA data.** A single good batch proves nothing. If a supplier cannot provide 5+ batch COAs with statistical consistency data, their process control is insufficient.
3. **Tg or Tm deviates from standard PEEK values.** Tg significantly below 143°C or Tm below 340°C indicates a PEEK copolymer, blend, or off-spec material — not the standard PEEK your application requires.
4. **Claims aerospace or medical certification without documentation.** Ask for the specific AMS number, the FAR 25.853 test report, or the ISO 10993 biocompatibility package. Vague claims of "aerospace grade" without traceable documentation are a disqualifier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Chinese PEEK the same polymer chemistry as Victrex or Syensqo PEEK?**
Yes. PEEK is a defined polymer chemistry — poly(oxy-1,4-phenyleneoxy-1,4-phenylenecarbonyl-1,4-phenylene). The base resin chemistry is identical regardless of producer. Differences exist in molecular weight distribution, purity, and additive packages — which is why qualification testing matters. But the fundamental polymer is the same.

**Can Chinese PEEK pass UL 94 V-0?**
Standard PEEK is inherently flame retardant — it achieves UL 94 V-0 at thin wall sections without any flame retardant additives. This is a property of the polymer chemistry, not the manufacturer. Verify the specific V-0 rating and wall thickness on the supplier's UL Yellow Card.

**What is the typical MOQ for Chinese PEEK?**
For trial/sample quantities: 5-25 kg. For production orders: 100-500 kg minimum, depending on grade and whether it is a standard or custom formulation. Custom color or specialty grades may have higher MOQs.

**How does Chinese PEEK pricing compare to Victrex?**
Chinese PEEK alternatives typically price 30-40% below Western incumbents on a CFR basis. Neat PEEK: $50-75/kg Chinese vs. $80-120/kg Western. The differential is structural — driven by manufacturing scale and feedstock economics — and has been stable for several years.

**Is Chinese PEEK suitable for medical devices?**
For non-implant medical applications (sterilization trays, surgical instrument handles, diagnostic equipment housings), Chinese PEEK with appropriate FDA clearance and ISO 10993 testing is suitable. For permanent implant applications (spinal cages, cranial plates), the regulatory pathway requires specific biocompatibility data packages and clinical history that most Chinese producers are still building. Verify the specific regulatory approvals for your device classification.
